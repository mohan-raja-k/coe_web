VISION & MISSION

Vision & Mission

Vision

“To emerge as a centre of excellence in research, innovation and incubation, transforming knowledge and ideas into impactful technologies, products and sustainable solutions for societal advancement.”

Mission

1

To foster a vibrant culture of innovative, collaborative and interdisciplinary research among faculty, students and researchers in emerging areas of science and engineering.

2

To translate research into innovative technologies, products and sustainable solutions through advanced infrastructure, prototyping, incubation, entrepreneurship and industry engagement.

3

To build strategic national and international partnerships with academic institutions, industries and research organizations for knowledge exchange, technology transfer and collaborative innovation.
