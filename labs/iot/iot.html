ABOUT THE CENTRE

About the Centre

The Centre of Excellence in Internet of Things promotes hands-on research in embedded systems, sensor networks and wireless communication. The centre supports the development and experimentation of connected devices, sensor-based monitoring systems and IoT applications for smart and automated environments.

  • Internet of Things
  • Sensor Networks
  • Embedded Systems
  • Connected Devices
  • IoT Communication

RESEARCH FOCUS

Research Focus of the Centre

01

Develop and test embedded systems through sensor interfacing, digital input/output control, UART communication and RF-based experimentation.

02

Design wireless sensor network applications using network topologies, peer-to-peer communication, IPv6 connectivity and 6LoWPAN.

03

Implement IoT-based environmental monitoring, remote sensor data transmission, IP-based lighting control and connected-device automation.
